  - **Relation to the Project**: This paper directly tackles the problem of predicting network latencies from incomplete and highly noisy data streams. The researchers explicitly evaluate their models using RIPE Atlas Round-Trip-Time datasets. It validates the approach of treating internet propagation delay as a combination of a predictable baseline structural trend and unpredictable residual noise, reinforcing why filtering outliers and targeting the baseline is the mathematically correct path.
